# Off-page SEO: authority, links, mentions, reputation

**Trigger:** `KHSEO offpage <site>`, "backlinks", "build authority", "link building".

Off-page signals tell search and AI systems that others trust you. KHSEO plans **earned**
authority only.

## 1. Baseline (Tier 1 data only, else `UNKNOWN`)
Referring domains and links (Search Console > Links, or Ahrefs/Semrush/DataForSEO/OpenSEO), top
linked pages, anchor-text mix, lost links, brand mentions, review profiles (Google Business
Profile, Trustpilot, marketplace reviews), and social profiles (consistent NAP and sameAs).

## 2. Tactics that are allowed and effective
| Tactic | How | Fits |
|---|---|---|
| Linkable assets | original data/surveys, calculators, free tools, templates, definitive guides, image galleries others can cite | all |
| Digital PR | newsworthy angles from your data or story → journalists, newsletters; answer journalist queries (e.g. source-request platforms) honestly | brands |
| Unlinked mentions | find mentions of the brand without a link → polite request | brands |
| Broken-link building | find dead resources on relevant sites → offer your equivalent | content sites |
| Partnerships | suppliers, collaborators, communities, events, associations, local organisations | local/B2B |
| Guest contributions | genuinely useful articles on relevant sites (not paid dofollow placements) | experts |
| Citations & directories | a few relevant, quality directories and consistent NAP; Google Business Profile for local | local |
| Reviews | ask real customers; respond to all reviews; never incentivize or fake | commerce/local |
| Social & community | platform-native content that earns visits, shares and natural links; forums/Reddit participation that adds value, not spam | all |
| AI visibility (GEO) | be cited in the sources AI assistants use: reputable lists, wikis/knowledge bases where you're eligible, consistent entity data | all |

## 3. Outreach rules
Personalize, give value first, disclose affiliations, respect "no", and don't mass-email. Paid
or sponsored links must be `rel="sponsored"` (or `nofollow`), per Google's link-spam policy.
**Sending outreach is an external action:** KHSEO drafts emails, and the user sends them (or
approves each send).

## 4. Never
Buying links for ranking · PBNs · link exchanges at scale · comment/forum spam · automated
link-building tools · fake reviews or review gating · negative SEO against competitors · expired-
domain redirects for authority. These risk manual actions and can wipe out rankings.
